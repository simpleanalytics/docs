You can change your plan yourself on [your plan page](https://dashboard.simpleanalytics.com/settings/plans). Select the plan you want and follow the steps. You need to be an owner, admin, or finance manager of the team to change the plan.

See our [pricing page](https://www.simpleanalytics.com/pricing) for the plans and what they include.

If the new plan has lower limits or fewer features than you use now, the plan page shows what we will delete (for example users, websites, custom views, or goals) before you confirm.

From November 1, 2026, websites on the Free plan need to show the Simple Analytics badge. Paid plans don't require the badge. Read more about the [Free plan badge](/free-plan-badge).
