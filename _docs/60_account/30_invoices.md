You can find your invoices in the Invoices section of your [account settings](https://simpleanalytics.com/account#invoices). This section is only visible to owners, admins, and finance managers of the team.

We offer you two ways to get your invoices:

1. One is to download them from your [account settings](https://simpleanalytics.com/account#invoices) and click on one of the past invoices.
1. Two is to receive them by email. Owners, admins, and finance managers always receive invoices. To also send them to another email address (for example that of your accountant), check "Please also send invoices to this email address", fill in the address, and click "Save". You will only get future invoices on that address.
