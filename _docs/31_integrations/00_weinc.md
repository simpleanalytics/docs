[WeInc](https://we.inc) is an AI website builder with a built-in Simple Analytics add-on. You don't need to paste any code: WeInc adds [our script](/script) to every page of your site when you publish.

Follow these steps to add Simple Analytics to your website:

1. Add your website's domain in [Simple Analytics](https://dashboard.simpleanalytics.com/). Our script recognises your site by its domain, so there is no ID to copy.
1. Open your site in the WeInc editor and open the **Add-ons** panel from the panels menu.
1. Find **Simple Analytics** in the list (under Analytics, or use the search box) and click it.
1. Check **Turn on** and click **Save**.
1. Publish your site. The script is added to every page when you publish, so the change goes live on your next publish.

After publishing, visit your site and check your dashboard to see the first page view come in.

If you encounter issues, don't hesitate to contact us via [our support channels](https://simpleanalytics.com/contact).
